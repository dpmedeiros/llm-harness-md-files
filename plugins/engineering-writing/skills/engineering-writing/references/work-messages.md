# Engineering work messages

Use [Reader context](../WRITING.md#reader-context) for the recipient's starting point, [Honesty](../WRITING.md#honesty) for evidence, and [Voice and wording](../WRITING.md#voice-and-wording) for tone and dates. Apply [Chat replies](../WRITING.md#chat-replies) when replying in the current chat.

## Address the recipient

When action is needed, say who should act, what they should decide or do, and the deadline if one is known. Preserve the supplied date's precision; a missing year or time zone needs clarification when it changes the meaning. Match the conversation's conventions, including email greetings or sign-offs where appropriate.

For review comments, distinguish a defect from a preference or open question.

## Illustrative review comment

> An empty batch reaches `events[0]` here, which raises an error before the importer returns. Could we return early for an empty batch?
