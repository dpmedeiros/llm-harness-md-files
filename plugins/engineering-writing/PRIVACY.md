# Engineering writing privacy policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026.

Engineering writing is a package of writing instructions, reference material, and assets maintained by Daniel Medeiros. This policy explains the plugin's data practices and the separate services you use to run it or request support.

## Using the plugin

The plugin has no developer-operated server, analytics, or telemetry. It does not automatically collect, send to the maintainer, or store your prompts, files, conversations, or generated writing. Installing or using the plugin does not give Daniel Medeiros access to your conversations.

Your AI application processes the material you provide according to its own terms, privacy policies, account settings, and workspace controls. When you use the plugin in ChatGPT or Codex, OpenAI's applicable policies and agreements govern that processing. See [OpenAI's privacy policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/) and any terms applicable to your account or organization.

The plugin adds no cookies, advertising trackers, or data collection code. The application running it and websites you visit have their own data practices.

## Public support on GitHub

Support is available through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/dpmedeiros/llm-harness-md-files/issues). When you open an issue or comment, the maintainer can see the information you choose to publish, including your GitHub username and the issue's text and attachments. These posts are public; include only information you are comfortable sharing publicly.

The maintainer uses support information to answer questions and maintain the plugin. Public support records may remain available on GitHub until they are edited or removed. To request removal of content the maintainer controls, open a support issue that identifies the content without repeating sensitive information.

GitHub hosts the repository, policy pages, and support issues. Its [General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) describes its collection, use, sharing, and retention of information. Other people may copy public content, and the maintainer does not control those copies or GitHub's systems.

## Questions and changes

For questions about this policy, contact Daniel Medeiros through the support link above. Keep sensitive information out of public issues. For information held by OpenAI or GitHub, use that provider's privacy controls and contact process.

Updates to this policy will appear on this page with a revised effective date.
